About Us

Rooted in Perambalur. Connected to the world.

Nandha Traders is a trading firm based in Perambalur, Tamil Nadu, active in the grain trade since 2000. With 25+ years of experience, we bring together Indian rice varieties and urad dal for food businesses, wholesalers, distributors and retailers.

From everyday staples to heritage grains, we help buyers explore varieties suited to their needs. Our approach is simple: thoughtful sourcing, clear communication and long-term relationships.
